# Code of Conduct

## Working together

Café is built through inclusive collaboration. Everyone who takes part
is expected to stay constructive: assume good intent, welcome
newcomers, give criticism that addresses the work rather than the
person, and respect disagreement about technical direction.

Harassment, threats, doxxing, hateful language, sexualized attention,
and any deliberate attempt to harm, silence, or exclude a participant
are not acceptable. Private information stays private: never publish or
demand someone's identity, contact details, location, or credentials.

## Reporting a concern

The repository has not been created remotely and no reporting URL
exists, so conduct concerns go through the existing private channel
agreed with the operator. Describe what happened without copying
private data into the report. Reports are handled discreetly and only
with the people needed to resolve them.

## Enforcement

Breaches are answered proportionally: a private warning for a first
minor lapse, temporary removal from collaboration for repeated or
serious misconduct, and permanent removal for threats, doxxing, or
sustained harassment. This project claims no staffed moderation
organization; the operator applies these steps as project work allows.
Decisions prioritize the safety of participants and the health of the
project.
